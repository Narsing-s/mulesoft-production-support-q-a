# 13 — Advanced L2/L3 Scenarios

## Slide 1 — High CPU
Compare before/after release. Inspect payload size, transformations, loops, concurrency and downstream latency.

## Slide 2 — Memory Pressure
Large payloads, excessive concurrency, inefficient transformations, excessive logging or insufficient runtime capacity.

## Slide 3 — Thread Starvation
Blocking operations, slow dependencies, excessive concurrency and resource contention.

## Slide 4 — Stakeholder Wants ETA
Never invent an ETA. Give impact, evidence, current action and next update checkpoint.

## Slide 5 — Recurring Incident
If restart repeatedly fixes the issue, investigate why it returns. Restart is temporary mitigation.

## Slide 6 — L2/L3 Mindset
L2: diagnose, recover and coordinate.
L3: deep RCA, architecture-level prevention, performance/resilience improvements and reusable fixes.
