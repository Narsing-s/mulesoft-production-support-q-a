## Slide 2 — Golden Production Answer
“First I confirm impact and scope. Then I collect the correlation ID and timestamp, inspect logs and metrics, isolate the failing boundary, validate configuration and dependencies, apply the safest approved mitigation, verify recovery, reconcile affected transactions and document RCA/prevention.”

## Slide 3 — Interview Rules
Speak from real experience. Do not expose confidential client information. Do not claim work you did not perform. Give concise answers first and technical depth when asked.

## Slide 4 — Final Mindset
Detect → Isolate → Mitigate → Verify → Reconcile → Prevent.
