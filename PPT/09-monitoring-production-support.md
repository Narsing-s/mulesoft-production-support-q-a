# 09 — Monitoring & Production Support

## Slide 1 — Monitoring
Availability, error rate, latency, throughput, queue/file backlog, CPU/memory and business KPIs.

## Slide 2 — Correlation ID
Search correlation ID + timestamp + application + flow + error + downstream call.

## Slide 3 — P1/P2
P1: confirm impact, coordinate bridge, assign investigation streams, mitigate safely, communicate and preserve evidence.

P2: confirm impact, investigate within SLA, recover, reconcile and document RCA.

## Slide 4 — Restart Rule
Do not restart first unless the approved runbook requires immediate restoration. Capture evidence first when possible.
