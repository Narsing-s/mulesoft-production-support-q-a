# 05 — DataWeave

## Slide 1 — Core
DataWeave is MuleSoft's transformation language for JSON, XML, CSV and Java/object data.

## Slide 2 — Functions
map, mapObject, filter, filterObject, pluck, groupBy, orderBy, distinctBy and reduce.

## Slide 3 — Production Failures
Null/missing fields, unexpected types, schema changes, XML namespaces, malformed input and assumptions not covered by tests.

## Slide 4 — Optimization
Filter early, select required fields, avoid repeated traversal, keep transformations readable and consider streaming for large payloads.
