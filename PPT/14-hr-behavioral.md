# 14 — HR & Behavioral

## Slide 1 — Tell Me About Yourself
“I am a MuleSoft integration and production-support professional with around four years of IT experience and several years focused on MuleSoft. My work includes enterprise integration support, monitoring, incident troubleshooting, connectivity/authentication/data analysis, deployment validation and RCA.”

## Slide 2 — Why Change?
“I am looking for stronger growth opportunities where I can take on challenging MuleSoft integration and production-support responsibilities and deepen my L2/L3 skills.”

## Slide 3 — Why Production Support?
“I enjoy solving real production problems where the impact is immediate—analyzing evidence, restoring service safely and preventing recurrence.”

## Slide 4 — Why Hire You?
“I bring hands-on production-support thinking: incident ownership, structured troubleshooting, integration knowledge and clear communication.”

## Slide 5 — Strength
Structured troubleshooting under pressure.

## Slide 6 — Weakness
“I previously spent too long investigating alone. I improved by setting an investigation window and involving the right team earlier.”

## Slide 7 — Pressure
Prioritize impact/SLA, use evidence, communicate concise updates and avoid risky changes.

## Slide 8 — Questions to Ask
What does a typical L2/L3 incident look like? Which deployment and observability tools are used? How are RCA/preventive actions measured? What defines success in the first 90 days?
